---
title: Security and privacy
description: Protect sensitive production evidence and keep optimization authority bounded.
---

A feedback store can contain production inputs, model outputs, human corrections, and downstream outcomes. Treat it as a sensitive system of record.

## Minimum controls

1. Redact secrets, credentials, and unnecessary personal data before capture.
2. Use pseudonymous entity identifiers and store re-identification maps elsewhere.
3. Separate observability retention from training consent and training retention.
4. Restrict raw payload access; prefer aggregate findings and redacted samples.
5. Version models, prompts, policies, authorizers, and application releases.
6. Authenticate correction sources and validate their authority.
7. Never let user-controlled content directly rewrite prompts, rules, schemas, or training sets.
8. Sign or authenticate deployable artifact versions and deployment events.
9. Keep authorization, tool allowlists, risk policy, and evaluator code outside the learned artifact.

## Threat model

| Threat | Example | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback poisoning | Coordinated false ratings | Source trust, rate limits, entity breadth, anomaly detection, verified outcomes, review |
| Prompt injection | Transcript contains deployment instructions | Data-only parsing, structured proposals, isolated generation, policy gates, no ambient credentials |
| Cross-tenant leakage | Evidence or candidate crosses a namespace | Core namespace validation, row-level authorization, namespace-scoped deployment |
| Unsafe optimization | Latency improves by skipping safety checks | Independent hard constraints that cannot be traded away |
| Runaway changes | Optimizer repeatedly applies variants | Candidate budgets, stable deduplication, risk ceilings, rollout limits, cooldowns, stop switch |
| Evaluator capture | Proposer weakens the test that judges it | Independent evaluator ownership and separate candidate lifecycle |

## Regulated and high-impact use

For medical, employment, credit, legal, safety, or other high-impact decisions, retain accountable qualified humans, validate applicable regulatory obligations, and use domain-specific evaluation and monitoring. The framework alone is not evidence that a system is safe or compliant.

## Data deletion and training provenance

Every training record should retain a reference to its source evidence, validation state, consent basis, redaction version, and dataset manifests. This enables later exclusion, erasure, or relabeling without reconstructing the entire pipeline.
